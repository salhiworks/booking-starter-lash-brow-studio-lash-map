## Photographs — Pexels License

All 56 files in `public/photos/` (AVIF and WebP) are processed crops of photographs from
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/). **`PHOTOS.md` lists them file by file**: the Pexels photo, the
photographer and where each is used. No original downloads are included.

They are **sample imagery**: the site labels them as samples, nobody shown works at the fictional
business, and you are expected to replace them with your own photographs.
